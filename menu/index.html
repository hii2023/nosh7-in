Bowl FAQs

Do you have a high-protein bowl in Ahmedabad?

Yes. The NOSH7 High-Protein Bowl delivers 40g or more of protein per meal from extra low-fat paneer, legumes and seeds, and is delivered fresh daily across Ahmedabad. There is also a Weight-Loss Bowl with 30g or more protein for calorie-controlled fat loss.

Is there a salad bowl or diet bowl near me in Ahmedabad?

NOSH7 delivers fresh salad bowls, high-protein bowls, weight-loss bowls and fruit bowls daily across West Ahmedabad, including Satellite, Prahlad Nagar, Bodakdev, Vastrapur, Thaltej, SG Highway, Bopal and 25+ surrounding areas. Delivery is free within 5 km of the Satellite kitchen.

How many salad varieties are on the NOSH7 menu?

The menu rotates 20+ salad meal varieties each month across five bowl types, with a seasonal refresh so you rarely eat the same bowl twice in a week. Calories and protein are listed for every dish.

Are the bowls a full meal or a side salad?

Every NOSH7 bowl is a complete meal of around 400g, not a side salad. It combines vegetables, a real protein source, seeds and a fresh in-house dressing, so it keeps you full for hours.
